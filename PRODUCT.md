# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: field drivers — on their phones, importing CSV order sheets exported from another app that lacks an API, then optimizing the day's pickup route and driving it. They use the app while moving, often one-handed, in the cab of a vehicle.
Secondary: dispatchers/admins — at a desk, managing the order calendar, reviewing the day's plan, handling SOS alerts, and overseeing the fleet. Same app, different surfaces.

## Product Purpose
Automate ERTH box pickup scheduling and route optimization for drivers. The driver imports a CSV of the day's orders, the app optimizes the route and renders it on a native 3D map, then generates shareable live-tracking links so each customer can watch their pickup approach in real time. Success: a driver spends seconds planning the day instead of an hour, drives a sensible route, and the customer is impressed enough by the tracking link to remember the brand.

## Positioning
A self-hosted native 3D route-optimization tool with customer-facing live tracking — the 3D map and the tracking-link experience are what nothing else in this category does.

## Brand Personality
Striking, professional, experimental. A young, modern tool with a touch of cyberpunk — confident enough for serious work, bold enough to surprise. Dark, neon-tinged, owner-operated. The customer-facing tracking links should make the customer say "wow."

## Anti-references
Not Facebook. Not corporate enterprise dashboards. Not old, dated, or beige. Nothing that reads as a 2000s-era fleet-management console or a generic SaaS template. Avoid cluttered utility-first tools like ZEO Route Planner — function without personality.

## Design Principles
- **The tracking link is the product's handshake.** Every customer-facing surface should wow on first load — that impression is the marketing.
- **The 3D map is the signature.** Treat it as a hero, not a widget. Its craft is what separates this from every competitor.
- **Professional by day, experimental by night.** Bold and cyberpunk where the driver tolerates flair, calm and legible where the job is on the line.
- **Self-hosted means self-styled.** Owner-operated pride over template defaults. No design decision is "because that's how these apps look."
- **Optimize for the cab.** Mobile-first, one-thumb-friendly, glanceable in motion. The desk experience is the companion, not the lead.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Flair-forward. No hard WCAG target; prioritize visual impact and the "wow" factor. Standard reduced-motion respect where cheap, but no constraint that softens the experimental edge.
