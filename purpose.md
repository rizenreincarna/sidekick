# Purpose: Marie's Knowledge Base

## Goals
Build and maintain a persistent, interlinked knowledge base about Naz's life,
work, health, family, and projects. This wiki is Marie's long-term memory.

## Key Questions
- What does Naz need to remember or be reminded of?
- What processes, people, and preferences affect daily operations?
- How can knowledge compound over time without being re-discovered?

## Scope
Personal knowledge — not general research. Everything is anchored to Naz's context.
